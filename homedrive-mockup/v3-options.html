Homedrive v3 · UX options

Nine improvements for v3. For each one: what is wrong now, what every option has in common, and two or three options to pick from. Most mockups can be clicked. Pick one option per question with Choose; the bar at the bottom collects your choices so you can copy them.

1Markdown: a real preview, and page editing that works

"md file preview. editor mobile more stable"

Now:
  • Opening a .md file shows its raw text in the viewer.
  • A document made in Homedrive can get stuck in .md mode. Found while preparing this: pressing Enter twice on the page saves an empty paragraph as extra blank lines (A\n\n\n\nB). Read back, that is two paragraphs, so the editor thinks the page would change the file and only offers the source. The Page button is then disabled with no way out.
Same in every option
  • Bug fix: the page editor writes empty lines so they read back the same, and tests check that everything it writes reopens on the page.
  • Page is never a dead end: when switching would really rewrite the file (another app's style, * bullets, tables, extra blank lines), tapping it asks first (question 1b). Nothing is saved until you type.
  • Files with images or HTML stay in .md: the page editor would drop them. The dialog then only says why.
  • The preview uses the editor's page style. Links open in a new tab, raw HTML stays text and images aren't loaded, as in the editor.

2Back animations

"back animations"

Now: opening a folder zooms in from its card. Going back only has a small zoom-out of the folder card, in grid view only and only within 2 seconds of leaving it; list view has nothing, and the viewer and editor just disappear.
Same in every option
  • Plays on every way back: the phone's back button or gesture, the ← Back button, the breadcrumb and the "You are here" rail. Switching tabs or sidebar sections only fades, as now.
  • Grid and list view both get it; the viewer and the editor get a closing animation too.
  • At most 420 ms, transform and opacity only; nothing moves with reduce motion.
  • Never waits for the network: the parent folder is kept from when you opened it, so it draws at once.
A

Zoom back

Recommended

The exact reverse of opening. The folder's items gather back into the folder's card, which settles into its place in the parent. In list view the folder's name slides from the "You are here" rail back into its row. Closing a file shrinks it back into its thumbnail.

  • Matches the zoom-in you already have: you see where you came from.
  • Feels like one space you move in and out of.
  • When the folder's card is off screen (scrolled away), it falls back to a fade.
B

Slide, like a phone app

Going into a folder slides it in from the right over the current one; going back slides it out to the right while the previous page comes back from slightly left. A file opens by rising up and closes by sliding down. This replaces the zoom-in, so both directions match.

  • Familiar from iPhone and Android apps; the direction tells forward from back.
  • Always works, also when the card is off screen.
  • Drops the folder zoom from v1.
  • On a computer a sideways slide feels heavier than a zoom.

5Select several files

"multiple file selection"

Now: everything works on one item at a time: moving twenty photos is twenty times Move.
Same in every option
  • Computers: a check circle shows in the top-left corner of a card (start of a row) on hover; clicking it selects. Ctrl/⌘-click adds or removes, Shift-click selects a range, Ctrl/⌘+A selects all, Esc clears. Clicking the name or thumbnail still opens.
  • Phones: long-press an item to start selecting, then tap to add or remove. The + button hides while selecting. Back clears the selection (question 7).
  • Actions: Download (one .zip), Move, Star / Unstar, Delete (to the trash, with one Undo). Share, Rename and Details stay one at a time. In Trash: Restore and Delete forever.
  • Items you can't change are skipped and the toast says so: "3 moved. 2 skipped: you can only view them."
  • Also in Recent, Starred, Shared with me and search results. Drag and drop of the selection onto a folder is left for later.

7The back button in the installed app

"back button pwa better integration. when on homepage ask to close. when not on homepage the last step should always be home"

Now:
  • A file, info sheet, dialog or the editor isn't a step in the history: back leaves the whole folder behind them.
  • ← Back to Photos adds a new step instead of going back, so after it the phone's back button goes forward into the folder you just left.
  • Opened from the share sheet, a link or (v3) a notification, back closes the app instead of going Home. On Home, back closes the app at once.
Same in every option: the rules
  1. Back closes the top thing first: a menu, dialog or sheet, the info sheet, a selection, then the viewer, then the editor (which saves first).
  2. Then it goes back through the folders and pages you visited. ← Back to … in the page does the same as the phone's back when that is where you came from, so the two never disagree.
  3. When Homedrive was opened on another page (the share sheet's Save to Homedrive, a link, a notification), the last step back is always Home (My Files), never out of the app.
  4. On Home, back asks before closing (below).

A web app can't close itself from a button: browsers don't allow it for installed apps. So the app is closed by pressing back a second time. On iPhone, home-screen apps have no back button; the in-page Back button follows the same rules. In a normal browser tab nothing asks: back leaves the site as usual.

8Right-click menu on computers

"right click integration on desktop"

Now: right-clicking a file shows the browser's own menu (Save link as…, Inspect), and everything else goes through ⓘ.
Same in every option
  • Right-click on an item (also the menu key and Shift+F10) opens Homedrive's menu with what its info panel offers: Open, Share…, Download, Rename…, Move…, Star, Details, Delete. What you can't do is left out, not greyed.
  • On a selection (question 5) the menu has the actions for several items. Right-clicking an item outside the selection selects just that one first.
  • On empty space: New folder, Upload files, New document, New spreadsheet (where you can add), and Grid / List. In Trash: Restore, Delete forever, Details.
  • Arrow keys move, Enter picks, Esc or a click elsewhere closes; the menu flips to stay on screen. In text fields, the editor and the viewer the browser's own menu stays (copy, paste, spelling).
  • On phones nothing changes: long-press selects (question 5).

9Fill in and sign PDFs

"add pdf editing also to the v3" — fill in forms and sign; a signature on the page is enough (no eID or itsme)

Now: PDFs can only be looked at. To fill in a form or sign a contract you download it, use another app, and upload the result again.
Same in every option
  • In the viewer, a PDF gets Fill & Sign (the pencil). It opens the PDF full screen with the same page view as the viewer, zoom included (question 6).
  • Form fields in the PDF (text, check boxes, radio buttons, lists) are shown in blue and filled in where they are. On any PDF, also without fields, you can place text, a date, ✓ or ✗, your signature and your initials anywhere; placed items can be moved, resized and deleted.
  • The date is written the way your language writes it (13/11/2026 in Dutch, 11/13/2026 in English), and can be changed.
  • On saving, everything is written into the page (flattened): the result looks the same in every app and can't be changed by accident.
  • People who can edit the file save it in place (question 9d). People who can only view it can still fill it in and save their copy in My Files, for example a form that was shared with them.
  • The signature is a picture on the page, like Adobe Fill & Sign or a scanned signature, not a digital signature with eID or itsme. PDFs with a password and XFA forms open read-only with a note.
